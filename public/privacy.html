Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Hands-Off is a prototype launch-readiness scanner. This policy explains what this instance collects.

Information we receive

Feedback you submit may include your message, optional email address, rating, topic, and submission time. Scan records may include scan results and repository references needed to provide the service.

How we use information

We use feedback to improve Hands-Off and use scan information to return reports. We do not intentionally collect or display detected secrets. Do not submit passwords, tokens, or other sensitive information in feedback.

Storage and sharing

This prototype stores data locally in its application database. We do not sell personal information. If deployed with third-party hosting or GitHub integration, those providers process data under their own policies.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the project repository or the contact method provided by the operator of this deployment.
